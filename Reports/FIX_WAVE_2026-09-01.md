## What this wave was

Four fixes landed as code with no built corpus behind them. This wave built
that corpus and checked each fix against a real document, then re-measured

## Two defects the verification found, and closed

### R24's bit fields cited a page they are not printed on

The first measurement of the check above returned **27/28** on the register
map and **33/34** on the datasheet. In both, one register was wrong and it was
the same one: `R24` emitted `15:14, 13:12, 11:1, 0` — cell for cell correct —
and cited p.17 of `LMX1204_registermap.pdf` (p.47 of `lmx1204.pdf`), where
R22's near-identical field table prints. R24's table prints on p.19 (p.49).

A citation that is nearly right is the one a reader trusts without opening the
page. Two producers answer "what page is this table on" and they disagreed.

**Fix A — `pagemap.reconcile_table_pages` (`9706737`), structure stage 2.** A
table's page is the page its first row prints on. `row_pages` is measured
geometry on the `pdf_layout` path and, on the HTML path, a walk that turns the
page only on strictly better evidence; the section-wide cell-value search is
neither. Measured over the eleven parts: **3 tables of 158 that carry both a
page and pinned rows disagreed, and all three had the table page wrong** —
each verified against the PDF by locating its caption:

| Table | was | is |
|---|---|---|
| `Table 1-25. R24 Register Field Descriptions` (`LMX1204_registermap.pdf`) | p.17 | p.19 |
| `Table 6-6 LOGICLK Formats and Properties` (`lmx1204.pdf`) | p.19 | p.20 |
| `Table 8-2 Calculated Voltage Values` (`lmx1204.pdf`) | p.56 | p.57 |

That took the register map to 28/28 and left the datasheet at 33/34: the
`ti_html` path has no geometry, so the row walk starts from the wrong seed and
can only step forward one page at a time.

**Fix B — a captioned table is on the page its caption prints on (`414c957`),
structure stage 3.** A caption names *the table*; cell values name its shape.
Measured **before** the change over every captioned table in every document:
**118 carried a pinned page, 117 were already on the page their caption prints
on, and one was not.** So the change corrected exactly one table and confirmed
the other 117. A caption that prints on more than one page (a continued table)
decides nothing and the cell-value search still answers.

Measured **after** the rebuild: **123 captioned tables carry a page, 123 of
them on the page their caption prints on.** The count rose from 118 to 123
because caption anchoring pins tables the cell-value search could not —
AFE7950 went from 12/15 to 13/15 tables pinned.

Cost, in the tracked corpora: **one spec record each in AFE7950 and AFE7953**
moved page — `rec_s4_12_7_..._-t1-r0`, a stray footnote row (`Includes PCB and
cable losses.`) attached to `Figure 4-284`, from p.79 to p.89 and from p.74 to
p.82. Its old page was the page its *section* starts on; its new page is where
the figure it annotates prints. All 619 and 536 record ids are unchanged, and
no other record in either part moved.

## What a reader should not conclude

- **Bit-field precision being 100% is not a claim about recall.** 28 of the
  register map's 35 registers publish a field set. The other seven are
  recorded in `derive/registers.py`'s warnings and in
  `tests/integration/test_phase6_bitfields.py`: six hand over no field table
  the reconstruction gate will accept, and `R90` prints two fields claiming
  the same eight bits, which no reading can make true at once.
- **`reconcile_table_pages` gives a field its table's first page, not its own
  row's page.** Every field table in this corpus prints on one page, so the
  two are the same today. A field table that spanned a page break would cite
  the page it started on, and the fix for that is to read `row_pages` in
  `derive/bitfields.py` — not needed until such a table exists.
- **`dsa build` still prints `descriptions LLM` when the LLM call failed and
  the deterministic writer wrote the descriptions.** `pipeline.used_llm`
  records the *intent*, not the outcome. The published artifact is correct;
  the summary line is not. Recorded here rather than fixed, because it is a
  reporting defect with no effect on published output.
